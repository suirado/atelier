FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

Is it really 3D?

Yes. Every room is built in 3D with its walls, doors, windows and furniture. You can turn it around, look from above or stand inside at eye level, and switch to a 2D floor plan whenever you like.

Do I need any design or architecture experience?

Not at all. Atelier is made for complete beginners. If you can click and drag, you can design a room.

Why do I need an account?

Your account is where your projects live. It saves your work automatically, so you never lose a design and can open it from any computer.

Is it free?

Yes. Creating an account and designing your home are free.

Can I use the real measurements of my home?

Yes. Choose "Build your own" and type your room's width, length and ceiling height in metres. Furniture is shown at its real size, so you can check that everything fits.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. On a phone, drag with one finger to turn the room and drag a piece to move it. For bigger plans, a computer with a mouse is the most comfortable.

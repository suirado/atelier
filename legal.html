The small print, kept short

Privacy, terms & contact

Atelier is a student project. It runs entirely in your browser: there is no server that stores your plans or your account. Last updated 22 November 2026.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device. Your account (name, email) and your projects are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to us, because there is no "us" server to send it to.

  • Passwords are never stored. Only a salted PBKDF2-SHA-256 hash (150,000 rounds) is kept, the same method real sites use.
  • Deleting is real. Deleting your account in Account settings removes your account and all its projects from this browser. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.
  • Another device is another Atelier. Because everything is local, your projects do not follow you to another browser or computer.

Third parties. The typeface is kept with the site, so no font service sees you. The 3D engine (Three.js), the PDF maker and, when you measure rooms, Python (Pyodide) load from the jsDelivr network, and the site itself is served by GitHub Pages: like any web request, they see your IP address, never your plans or your account. Product links open IKEA or Leroy Merlin, whose own privacy policies apply there.

Terms of use

Atelier is free to use for planning your own home. Please use it fairly: don't try to break it for others, and don't present the example content as your own work.

  • Measurements are a guide. Always measure the real room and check a product's size with the store before you buy.
  • Prices can change. Prices were checked on ikea.com/fr and leroymerlin.fr in November 2026. The store's own page is what counts.
  • No guarantee. The site is offered as it is, as a learning project. Keep a copy of anything important.

3D models and textures come from Poly Haven under the CC0 licence; see Credits.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA. Here is what that means today:

  • Text and buttons meet 4.5:1 contrast in both light and dark mode.
  • Everything in the planner works with a keyboard: arrows move pieces, Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Animations calm down when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Furniture and decor pins on the home page use different shapes, not only different colours.

Known limits. The 3D view itself can't be read by a screen reader; the 2D plan and the lists beside it carry the same information. If something stops you, please tell us (see Contact).
